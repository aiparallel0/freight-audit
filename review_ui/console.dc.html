Freight invoice audit

Stop paying freight bills you should be disputing.

Every carrier invoice gets reconciled against the rate confirmation and the proof of delivery before a dollar goes out. We catch the overcharges, duplicates, and unauthorized accessorials, and we surface the detention revenue you are quietly owed.

Catch the overpayment

Overcharges against the agreed rate, duplicate line items, accessorials over their cap, and detention billed that the POD cannot substantiate. Flagged before the bill is paid, not after.

Recover what you are owed

The reverse case nobody checks: when the POD proves a long wait but the carrier billed no detention, that is your revenue left uncollected. We surface it so you can bill it.

Frequently asked
How does the audit actually work?
Each invoice is reconciled to the cent against the rate confirmation and the proof of delivery. Every gap becomes a finding with a dollar impact and a severity.
Do I have to change my TMS?
No. Decisions export to QuickBooks IIF, a generic TMS JSON payload, or plain CSV, so the audit fits the system you already run.
What about scanned paper?
The OCR pipeline turns a photo into structured data and self checks it. Low confidence fields are routed to a human before they ever reach the audit.
How long is onboarding?
Your tolerances, caps, and fuel formula are a JSON profile, not a code change. Most accounts are auditing real loads within a day.
Is my data isolated?
Every account is scoped to its own data and an append only audit log records who approved what and when. Everything shown here is synthetic.
What does it cost?
A free 30 day pilot, then a flat monthly plan by volume. See pricing for the tiers.
Start auditing this week.
Run your own loads through a free pilot. No change to your TMS.
All figures and loads shown are synthetic sample data. No real carrier, broker, or shipment is represented.
